# clocks: Two clocks for the price (week's level and today's shift)

## Why we tried it
The tracker loses a day around every price war by either freezing or chasing it down, breaking pricing for the next morning. It takes small steps on a multiplier that mixes a slow-moving level with daily shifts.

## What changed
Added Two Clocks fields in Params:
- `morning_reset`: False
- `price_lr_day0`: 0.15
- `war_shift`: -0.12
- `shift_cap`: 0.30
- `outage_shift`: False

When `morning_reset` is True, `shift` starts at 0 each morning. The learning rate starts at `price_lr_day0` and decays to `price_lr`. A shift under `war_shift` prevents it from being folded into `level_mult`. When `outage_shift` is True, a region hitting 8 wins in a row detects an outage and applies its own upward shift capped at +25%.

Run with: `uv run lab eval -s morning_reset=True -s outage_shift=True -s price_lr_day0=0.1 -s war_shift=-0.12 --name clocks-dev-outage-true`
