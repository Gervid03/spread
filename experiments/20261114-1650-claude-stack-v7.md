## What is left out, and why
- **cp-chains** (+€341 on v5, t 2.75): its hitch (two cars, one driver) and return offers are a second implementation
  of what cp-routes does with `net_relay` and `net_offer`, each with its own bookkeeping of the same orders and gigs.
  With both on they would pair and offer the same cars twice. cp-routes measured better, so the stack takes it. A port
  of what is unique in cp-chains (the chain guard: no chains while projected on-time < 0.955 or >= 2 urgent orders
  are open) onto the routes code is open work.
- cp-margin's `min_margin_cents`, cp-volume's slow-option fee multiples (`w3d_expected_fee_mult`,
  `slow_expected_fee_mult`, `slow_comp_slope`) and the `won_return_hub` stat of cp-wild: their code collided with
  cp-demand's floor and target logic, and none is in its branch's winning flags, so v6's version was kept; the
  params exist but do nothing.

## Merge notes (for whoever debugs it)
- cp-margin's `self.day_open_mult` (a number, for `war_morning_reset`) clashed with the supervisor's
  `self.day_open_mult` (a table by day): renamed `self.war_open_mult`. Without it the supervisor would crash on day 1.
- cp-wild's future same-day block moved below the deadline computation; it now also respects cp-routes' relay and
  net-thin same-day blocks and the supervisor's `eff_ret_*` and `eff_sd_supply` dials.
- cp-volume's trade-in slack uses the supervisor's `eff_min_slack`; `ready` stays cp-routes' `ready_pre` when it has
  one. Its rejoin `elif` was put back on its own loop.
