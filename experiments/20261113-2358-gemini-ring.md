# gemini-ring: The ring: gate on who can reach the car, not on who is notified

- **Agent / branch / commit:** gemini / exp/gemini-ring
- **Date:** 2026-11-13 23:58
- **Idea from:** IDEAS.md item 1

## Why we tried it
The app notifies drivers within 30 km of the first pickup, but drivers browse further and book what pays them. For a driver in the Helsinki ring, Hyvinkää → capital even ends at home, so it costs less than the typical fee assumes. We were unnecessarily declining requests from the ring (like Hyvinkää) due to their low driver count within 30 km.

## What changed
Added two new parameters to `Params` in `src/spread_lab/policy.py`:
- `gate_radius_km` (default 0): The straight-line radius of the supply count that the decline gate uses.
- `far_options` (default "all"): With "slow", quote `next_day` and `within_3_days` only for pickups that pass only on the wider radius.
- Tracked `won_ring`, `late_ring`, and `margin_ring_cents` in `Desk.stats`.
Run with: `uv run lab eval -s gate_radius_km=55 -s far_options=slow --name gemini-ring`
