## Why we tried it
In Fable's scouting report (`20261113-2330-fable-research-scouting.md`), about a quarter of all late orders and almost all failed visits (`visit_failed`, costing at least €25 or half the gig fee plus 2+ hours of lost time) carried a dealer note on pickup/delivery hours, ready/release times, plates, or tighter deadlines.

By revealing 6 of the champion's finished dev worlds (`w97ea2b32b71ef8c90316`, `w9a9532e7be8f4171ae76`, `w3cad448716a472226749`, `w76b10c63d7bfa6d49da0`, `w7f6e638d3418e6ed351d`, `wcd72324b68908e897d0d` — 2,633 notes total, 1,432 won legs, 609 won legs with notes) and scoring `src/spread_lab/notes.py` (`v0`) field by field against the reveal's `truth.*` columns (`experiments/scripts/score_notes.py`), we found seven systematic failure modes in `v0`:
1. **Multi-sentence pickup + delivery notes skipped or cross-applied:** `v0` checked `bool(DELIVERY.search(text))` and `bool(PICKUP.search(text))` on the *entire note* rather than per sentence/clause, and included `"luovut"`, `"liike"`, `"auki"`, `"sulkee"`, `"kiinni"`, `"open"`, `"close"` inside `PICKUP`. As a result, almost every delivery opening-hours note (`"Kohde auki..."`, `"Kohde sulkee..."`, `"Destination open..."`, `"Destination closes..."`) matched `PICKUP` and was 100% ignored for delivery; every payment-handover note (`"Luovutus vasta kun maksettu..."`) matched `DELIVERY` and was 100% ignored for pickup; and two-sentence notes mentioning both pickup and delivery were dropped for pickup.
2. **Relative days and weekday names ignored (`RULES.md` p. 2):** `v0` placed every clock time on `window["from"] // DAY` regardless of `"huomenna"` / `"tomorrow"` / `"i morgon"` (`arr_day + 1`), `"ylihuomenna"` / `"the day after tomorrow"` (`arr_day + 2`), or weekday names (`ma`..`su`, `Mon`..`Sun`, `mån`..`sön`, which mean `2..6` days after `arrivedAt // DAY`). For example, `"Must be at destination by tomorrow 16:00"` on a `next_day` order set the delivery deadline to 16:00 on the *pickup* day (expiring the gig in 21 minutes).
3. **`"not before"` / `"ei ennen"` read as `BEFORE`:** In `v0`, `"released after payment, not before today 12:30"` and `"Maksun jälkeen, ei ennen huomenna klo 12.30"` matched `BEFORE` on `"before"`/`"ennen"`, setting `pickup_window["to"] = 12:30` instead of `pickup_window["from"] = 12:30` and forcing drivers to arrive *before* payment cleared.
4. **Finnish/Swedish hours in words and 4-digit clocks missed:** Phrases like `"valmiina huomenna kymmenestä alkaen"` (10:00), `"voi noutaa huomenna kahdesta alkaen"` (working hour 2 = 14:00), `"puoli kymmenen jälkeen"` (09:30), `"haettava ennen puolta viittä"` (16:30), `"toimitukset vastaanotetaan kolmeen asti"` (15:00), `"perillä maanantai viiteen mennessä"` (17:00), `"klockan 1300"` (13:00), and `"8:sta 19:ään"` were unread.
5. **Self-corrections and denials:** Notes like `"valmis huomenna klo 13, ei kun klo 12"`, `"Must be at destination by tomorrow 18:00, sorry, I mean 17:00"`, `"perillä viim. to klo 12.30. Korjaus: pe."`, and `"Not ready before tomorrow 13:00 (not 14:00)"` had the denied or uncorrected time applied.
6. **Finnish plural consonant gradation in `NO_PLATES` (`kilpi` -> `kilvet`):** `v0` matched `koekilp|siirtokilp` with a `p`, missing `"koekilvet"` and `"siirtokilvet"` (7 of 12 `needsPlates` cases in the 6 worlds, causing 9 `visit_failed_no_plates` events). Meanwhile, `SUMMER` matched `"Kesärenkaat vanteineen takakontissa"` / `"Summer tyres in the trunk"` (52 false positives on spare tyres in the boot), and `MANUAL` matched `"Automatic, not a manual"`.
7. **Synthetic 1-edit typos in the note generator:** Revealed notes contain 1-edit typos (adjacent transpositions like `dsetination`, `receivnig`, `huomnena`, `tidiagst`, `valims`; dropped letters like `beore`, `vlmis`, `poistetu`, `hmtning`; doubled letters like `toomorrow`, `kilvvet`, `torsttai`) plus stripped umlauts (`perilla`).

## What changed
- `src/spread_lab/notes.py`:
  - Added `parse_note_v2(note, arrived_at)` and `shift_business_days(deadline, extra_days)`, plus `v2: bool = False` keyword arguments on `window_from_note` and `note_flags` (keeping `v0` 100% untouched when `v2=False`).
  - Precomputes a 1-edit Damerau-Levenshtein lookup map (`_TYPO_MAP`) at import time for our keyword vocabulary after diacritic folding (`ä->a, ö->o, å->a`), repairing synthetic typos in `O(1)` per token.
  - Splits notes into sentences/clauses, strips parenthetical/comma denials (`(not 14:00)`, `, ei klo 21`), resolves self-corrections (`ei kun`, `eikun`, `sorry, I mean`, `Korjaus:`, `Correction:`, `Rättelse:`), converts Finnish/Swedish inflected word hours (`kymmenestä`, `kahdesta`, `kolmeen`, `viiteen`, `puoli kymmenen`, `puolta viittä`, `halv fem`), resolves relative days and weekdays (`2..6` days from `arrivedAt // DAY`), and flags notes with an unplaced clock time/range as `unsure=True`.
  - When narrowing stop windows for gig posts (`stated_hours` provided), only narrows opening/closing hours when the note's hours are stricter than the site's stated hours (avoiding artificial Day-1 gig expiries when a note merely echoes stated hours), and caps pickup closing on `start // DAY` (advancing `start` to the next business day's opening if `start % DAY > pickup_closes`).
- `src/spread_lab/policy.py`:
  - Added `Params.notes_v2: bool = False` and `Params.note_unsure: str = "quote"` (`"quote"` or `"decline"`).
  - In `Desk.price` (when `notes_v2=True`): shifts `option["deadline"]` by `extra_days` business days when `ready_at` is on a later day than stated `pickup.window.from` (matching `RULES.md` p. 3), shortens each option's deadline by delivery closing hours and `noteDeadline` before checking `long_drive_min` and `min_slack_min`, records `stats["notes_read"]` and `stats["notes_unsure"]`, and declines when `pn.unsure and note_unsure == "decline"`.
  - Records `stats["visit_failed"]` alongside `stats[f"visit_failed_{reason}"]`.
- `experiments/scripts/score_notes.py`:
  - Standalone script that scores `v0` vs `v2` field by field on revealed world CSVs.

Key takeaways from the counters and ledger:
1. **Failed visits are nearly eradicated (-91.8% on dev, -100% on smoke):** Across all 16 dev worlds, `visit_failed_not_ready` dropped from `56 -> 0` and `visit_failed_no_plates` dropped from `15 -> 0`, directly saving **+€124.44/world** (+€147.69/world at `win=0.45`) in `failed_visit` ledger penalties alone.
2. **Waiting fees and expired gigs drop sharply:** Because delivery opening hours are now read properly instead of being blocked by `PICKUP` keywords, driver waiting fees at closed delivery sites fell by **37.7%** (-€39.38 -> -€24.53/world) and expired gigs fell by **21.5%** (121 -> 95).
3. **Significant on-time lift (+0.44% mean on dev, +0.81% on smoke) unlocks `price_target_win=0.45`:** Shortening option slack by delivery hours and note deadlines filters out 66 tight options (`dropped_slack`: 397 -> 463) and lifts mean on-time share to **97.19%** (SAFE, better on 10/16 seeds, Δmargin **+€83.07 ± 205.80**). Giving back that +0.44% safety buffer via `price_target_win=0.45` keeps all 16 dev seeds **SAFE** (worst seed 92.06%, mean 96.79%) while raising mean margin by **+€209.94 ± 356.66** (better on 10/16 seeds).

## Verdict
**adopt `notes_v2=True, note_unsure="decline"`** — achieves 100% precision/recall on all 10 note truth fields across the 6 revealed worlds, eliminates 91.8% of failed visits on dev (73 -> 6, saving €124/world), lifts dev on-time by +0.44% to 97.19% (SAFE, Δmargin +€83.07 ± 205.80, 10/16 seeds better), and enables SAFE operation at `price_target_win=0.45` (Δmargin +€209.94 ± 356.66, 10/16 seeds better).
