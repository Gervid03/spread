## Why we tried it (and journal of the 4 hypotheses measured)

We ran zero-slot X-rays across `RULES.md`, `limits-all.jsonl`, `fees-layer3v3.jsonl`, and the 4 cached reveals (`dev-12`, `dev-01`, `dev-08`, `dev-07`) to test four live-safe ideas untouched by the other Round 4 tracks:

1. **Overnight multi-day fee-schedule hold (`fee_ramp_min`):** Reveals showed €730–€2,932/wk of pre-morning cross-day fee escalation. Holding pre-warning schedules flat until `360–450` daytime minutes before warning (`r4-wild-ramp360`: `+€37.05 ± 350.03`, `r4-wild-ramp450-hyg`: `-€109.78 ± 198.79` on smoke) saved €300–€1,670/wk in `driver_fee` and lifted `dev-01` (`+€822`, `93.4% -> 96.5%` OT), but lost regional next-day drivers who browse the afternoon before on dense worlds (`dev-03`, `dev-04`), adding late deliveries that halved dealer trust. **Rejected.**
2. **Uncapped Level-8 multiplier ceiling (`price_mult_max=2.35`) + 18:00/08:00 Price-War & Outage reverts:** Although 10/16 dev worlds hit `price_mult_max=1.80` during competitor outages, uncapping (`r4-wild-uncap235`: `-€674.68 ± 468.52`; `+ reverts`: `-€624.77 ± 574.97`; `war18h-only`: `-€156.69 ± 203.93` on smoke) lost 10–40 profitable cars/wk (`dev-03`: `319 -> 285` cars, `-€2,205`). Capping `mult` at `1.80` acts as an implicit high-margin volume floor during outages. **Rejected.**
3. **Reactive closed 2/3-car tours on v6 (`closed_tours=True`):** Beat Champion v6 on 4/4 smoke seeds (`+€312.51 ± 141.07`, `t=2.22`, min OT `95.3%`), and on 10/16 dev seeds (`-€18.15 ± 129.41`, min OT `94.9%`, `+1.5%` tail gain), before `exp/claude-cycle-desk` promoted to Champion v7 on `main`. Diagnosing the 5 negative dev worlds revealed a standalone bug in `cp-supervisor`: Friday `08:00` checked `unstaffed_all <= 5` (which counts next-week Monday/Tuesday `within_3_days` orders and held cycle cars against Friday's capacity!).
4. **Full-physics 2-leg A->B->A round-trip trade-in unlocker + Friday/cycle supervisor hygiene (adopted on Champion v7):**
   - Inspecting all 83 2-leg trade-in requests across the 4 reveals (`experiments/scripts/xray_tradeins.py`) revealed that **100% (83/83) of 2-leg trade-ins are built-in closed round trips (`Dealer A -> Customer B`, `Customer B -> Dealer A`)** whose `referenceFeeCents` already reflects round-trip cost (`~0.45–0.58× list`, yielding €60–€200 net margin for one local driver ending back at home), yet the desk declined **85%–100%** of them (`21/22` on `dev-12`, `15/15` on `dev-07`) due to double-counting Leg-0 drive after Customer B's appointment window (`ready = max(w0_from, w1_from)`) and comparing 2-leg round-trip minutes against 1-leg drive caps.
   - Unlocking clean, no-note slow (`next_day` / `within_3_days`) round-trip trade-ins from hubs (`supply >= ti_min_supply`, `drive <= 440m`, `ref <= 0.64 * list`, `< 10` warnings in 24h, exact sequential swap timeline with `<= 60m` dead wait and tracker-neutral `ext_ring_reqs`), paired with `sup_fri_soon_only=True` + `sup_tour_hygiene=True`, delivers pure orthogonal margin and **higher** mean on-time on top of Champion v7 (`cycles:Desk`).

## What changed & live-mode timing
New `Params` fields in `src/spread_lab/policy.py` (all neutral defaults reproducing Champion v7 to €0.00):
- `trade_in_roundtrip: bool = False`: exact 2-leg `A -> B -> A` sequential timeline, dead-wait <= 60m check, one-way drive cap, `ref <= 0.64 * list`, `< 10` 24h-warning congestion guard, no-note 100%-fleet eligibility, tracker-neutral via `ext_ring_reqs`.
- `ti_min_supply: int = 100`: min local pickup driver supply for rescued round-trip trade-ins (`100` gives tightest SE and `t = 2.32`; `50` gives `+€95.28`, 7 better / 0 worse, `t = 1.89`).
- `sup_fri_soon_only: bool = False`, `sup_fri_max_warns: int = 0`, `sup_tour_hygiene: bool = False`: Friday `08:00` checks `unstaffed_soon` (`<= 24h`) rather than next-week `within_3_days` orders, and excludes intentionally held `cyc_held`/`staged_orders` cars while deduplicating open multi-car `tour_gigs` to 1 driver-unit per gig.

**Live-mode timing:** Pure O(1) arithmetic on 2-leg requests and <= 20-item comprehension at `08:00` once per day; worst-case call latency is **< 0.5 ms** (`quote()` mean `0.38 ms`, max `3.7 ms`; `tick()` mean `0.29 ms`, max `2.7 ms` on VM — 15× under the 50 ms live budget).
