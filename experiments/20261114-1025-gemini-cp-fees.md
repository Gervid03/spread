## Why we tried it
Driver fees consume ~63–68% of revenue (`€-19,074` of `€30,474` on 16 dev worlds; `€-22,044` of `€32,574` = **67.7%** on `dev-09`). Before spending a single practice slot, we exported and x-rayed the finished Champion v4 reveal of `dev-09` (`w7b16f01aa028c742ea03`: `€10,392.04` margin, `293` won, `99.32%` on time, `8,712` gig views, `309` gigs) alongside the exact driver-learning rules in `HANDBOOK.md` ("Drivers learn your fees"):

1. **Expectation poisoning & app hold-out (`HANDBOOK.md` formula):** Whenever a gig is booked, *every* eligible driver who viewed that order learns `booked_fee / first_viewed_fee` and `booked_at - first_view_at`. On `dev-09`, non-warn single-car bookings jumped from **`0.905×` typical fee on Monday** (median `0.850×`, only 4 gigs `>1.05×`) to **`1.004×` on Tuesday** (`26% > 1.05×`) and stayed near `~0.99×` all week. Across 6,970 order-viewer exposures on `dev-09`, our rising/urgent schedules taught drivers a viewer-weighted raise ratio of **`1.321×`** — led by individual gigs teaching `3.29×` to 83 viewers (`g256`), `3.00×` to 46 viewers (`g276`), and `1.84×` to 143 viewers (`g197`). As a result, 58 bookers on `dev-09` viewed a gig early at `0.928×` and held out to book the *same* gig later at `1.132×` (`+0.204×`), while 52 of the 70 gigs booked at `1.00–1.65×` already had at least one local/homeward viewer (`<= 35 km` from pickup or delivery) in the first 30 minutes.
2. **Direct offers to post-15m local/homeward viewers break hold-out (`view_offer_mult`):** Per `HANDBOOK.md`, *"A driver who reads an offer takes it when the fee pays for the gig and what else their day is worth to them. In the app the same driver may hold out for more, because the fee may rise there."* Crucially, notifications fire at `t = 0..4` min to casual openers, whereas any eligible driver living within `35 km` of pickup or delivery who views an unbooked gig at `t >= 15` min is actively browsing or returning for their planned second look (filtering out curiosity browsers `> 35 km` away). Answering that view (`2..15` min old) with a direct offer at `1.03×` the current board fee (capped at `1.15×` reference fee) clears the gig immediately without board escalation and teaches all viewers a near-`1.0×` raise ratio.
3. **Stale schedule escalation while booked (`drop_reprice_bump`):** On `dev-09`, 31 completed gigs were booked at a mean of **`0.906×`**, held for hours (`median 180 min`), and then dropped (`gig_dropped`). Because `Desk` left the original rising schedule running while the gig was off the market, those gigs woke up at **`1.136×` (up to `1.55×`)** and rebooked at **`1.172×`** (`€690` extra paid on `dev-09` alone, plus teaching 143 viewers `1.84×` on `g197`). Repricing non-urgent dropped gigs (`>= 60 min` before warning) back to `clamp(last_booked_mult + 0.03, fee_open, fee_warn)` stops overnight drop inflation.
4. **Private-seller rebooking & overnight `steep()` bug (`urgent_hygiene` + monotonic `steep`):** Event-sequence tracing of `R00698` (`g256`, paid **`€536.98 = 2.80×`** on a `€262` `within_3_days` order delivered 4 days before deadline!) and `R00212` (`g61`, paid **`€298.23 = 2.80×`**) uncovered a structural bug: when a private seller's booked pickup slot expires, `order_changed` arrives *before* `gig_expired` with a new `lastPickupAt` 24 hours later, yet `order["id"]` remained permanently stuck in `self.urgent`, posting the next-day gig on `steep()` (`1.80× -> 2.80×` climbing every 30 minutes *through the night* while drivers sleep). Clearing `self.urgent` on `order_changed` when `lastPickupAt - now > 3h`, marking orders whose new pickup window + road drive already exceeds `deadline` as `lost`, restricting `steep()` steps to daytime (`07:00–22:00`), repricing unbooked open gigs in place to `late_fee_open` (`1.0×`) after `lastPickupAt + 60 min` (without stripping windows), and setting a monotonic post-warning rescue schedule (`steep_open=1.65, steep_step=0.20, steep_max=2.25` above `fee_warn=1.60`) eliminates `2.80×` expectation-poisoning spikes while preserving full rescue power on severe shortage worlds.

## What changed
Added neutral-default `Params` fields and `rejoin()`-safe state/telemetry in `src/spread_lab/policy.py` (`cp-fees-noop` verified exact `Δmargin €+0.00 ± 0.00` on smoke). The promoted candidate enables 8 flags on top of Champion v4:
- `-s view_offer_mult=1.03 -s view_offer_cap=1.15` (`view_offer_after_min=15`, `view_offer_window_min=15`): when an open gig is `>= 15` minutes old during `07:00–21:00` and an eligible non-busy driver living within `35 km` of first pickup or last delivery viewed that gig `2..15` minutes ago without booking, sends a direct offer at `min(1.15 × base, 1.03 × current_board_fee)`.
- `-s drop_reprice_bump=0.03`: on `gig_dropped` / `no_show` of a non-urgent, non-lost gig with `>= 60` minutes left before `lastPickupAt - 3h`, reprices the gig to a fresh daytime schedule starting at `clamp(last_booked_mult + 0.03, fee_open, fee_warn)` whenever that saves `> 0.03×` vs the stale schedule.
- `-s urgent_hygiene=True -s late_reprice_grace_min=60`: (a) clears `self.urgent` on `order_changed` when `lastPickupAt - now > 3h` and marks orders whose earliest pickup + remaining road drive exceeds `deadline` as `lost` (rebuilt identically in `rejoin()`); (b) restricts `steep()` raises after the initial step to daytime hours (`day_start..day_end`); (c) reprices unbooked open gigs in place down to `late_fee_open` (`1.0×`) once `now > lastPickupAt + 60 min` without withdrawing or stripping windows.
- `-s steep_open=1.65 -s steep_step=0.20 -s steep_max=2.25`: strictly monotonic escalation above `fee_warn=1.60` at `deadline_close` (`1.65 -> 1.85 -> 2.05 -> 2.25`), avoiding both the `1.60 -> 1.45` dip that hurt `dev-07` in `dev1` and Champion's `2.80×` expectation-poisoning ceiling.
