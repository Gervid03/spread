# r4-volume: Surge-guarded regional inbound volume, benign-note sequential 2-leg trade-ins, 20 km metro pairing, and cycle-hold supervisor unblocking on Champion v7

- **Agent / branch / commit:** gemini (`r4-volume`) · `exp/gemini-r4-volume` · see branch HEAD
- **Date:** 2026-11-14 19:40 Helsinki (17:40 UTC)
- **Idea from:** Round 4 direction `r4-volume` ("more cars per driver-hour, not just cheaper quotes") + own X-rays on Champion v6/v7 (`xray_r4_volume.py`, `xray_capacity_deep.py`, `xray_driver_km.py`, `xray_batches_and_floors.py`, `r4-vol-diag` co-play on `dev-12`)

## Why we tried it
Round 6–7 leaderboard teams win 410–460 orders/week at roughly our margin per order while Champion v6 (`285.9/wk`, `€11,874`) and Champion v7 (`spread_lab.policies.cycles:Desk`, `284.1/wk` delivered / `286.9/wk` won, `€12,121.69` on `dev`) decline ~275 requests/week and pair few non-cycle corridor cars. Slot-free X-rays across all 16 dev results and 4 TAS worlds (`dev-12`, `dev-01`, `dev-07`, `dev-08`) plus a 2-turn co-play week (`r4-vol-diag` on `dev-12`) identified four orthogonal, driver-neutral or driver-saving bottlenecks that remain untouched in Champion v7:

1. **Unbacked vs. destination-backed regional inbound returns & extended ring (`return_hub_min_supply: 200 -> 45`, `return_hub_max_drive_min: 180 -> 270`, `ext_ring_max_drive_min: 220 -> 260`, guarded by `r4v_guard_return_hub=True`):**
   - Champion v7 only admits `<22`-supply slow return orders into Capital (`supply >= 200`, `drive <= 180m`), declining `35–50` slow requests/week heading into regional hubs (`Oulu 90`, `Tampere/Lempäälä 60`, `Turku/Raisio 52`) or medium-haul Capital returns (`181–270m`). Earlier unguarded `cp-volume` dipped on `night-11`/`night-15` because thin intake stayed open during intraday stress; adding an intraday circuit breaker (`_r4v_surge_safe`: `sup_regime != "stressed"`, open unstaffed urgent orders `< 4`, `projected_on_time() >= 0.942`) snaps gates back to `200 / 180 / 220` (`213.6` guarded requests/wk on `dev`) whenever capacity tightens.
2. **Benign-note sequential 2-leg trade-ins (`r4v_tradein_seq=True`, `r4v_tradein_max_drive=450`):**
   - Two-leg trade-ins (`Dealer -> Private Customer -> Dealer`) are built-in closed 2-car round-trips (`0` empty return km, `0` return transit ticket, `+€55–€95` margin/order), yet `policy.py` computed `ready = max(now, leg0.from, leg1.from)` from the customer's late swap slot and declined `18/19` gate-passing trade-ins on `dev-12` (`€2,227` surplus). Crucially, inspecting `dev-12` trade-in notes (`check_d12_ti.py`) caught why `R00606` (`"vaihtari tulee takaisin meille, nouto 10 - 15"`) caused 7 `visit_failed_closed` penalties (`-€488`): a multi-leg note with narrowed hours applies to the dealer return too. Requiring benign notes (`no narrowed hours/deadlines/ready_at/unsure/summer_tyres`), `supply >= 22`, `w1.from - now >= 240m`, and sequential post-swap slack `>= 75 + 0.35 * drv1` rescues clean trade-ins while reducing `failed_visit` (`€-28.49 -> €-18.78/wk`).
3. **Expanded `20 km` metro, benign-note, and cross-slow 2-car pairing on un-cycled solo releases (`lead_car_max_gap_km=20.0`, `r4v_lead_benign_notes=True`, `r4v_lead_cross_slow=True`):**
   - In Champion v7, ~87% of `cyc_held` slow cars do not find a closed cycle within 120 daytime minutes and release solo via `super(TasRoutesDesk, self).post()`. Allowing benign notes (`72%` of slow notes!), `20 km` metro twin pickups (`Helsinki-Espoo-Vantaa`, `Tampere-Lempäälä`, `Turku-Raisio`), and `next_day + within_3_days` pairing at `1.0x` (`0.85x` opening) lifts `lead_car_booked` from `17.81 -> 24.12/wk` (`+6.31` 2-car gigs/wk = `+12.6` cars/wk moved two-at-a-time).
4. **Cycle-hold supervisor unblocking (`r4v_cyc_unblock=True` / `r4v_cyc_unblock_backlog`):**
   - Per-seed diffs (`diff_dev1_outliers.py`) revealed that cars held voluntarily off the board overnight in `cyc_held` / `staged_orders` were counted as unstaffed congestion at the 08:00 `maybe_supervise()` check, falsely flipping calm/Friday-surplus days into `"stressed"` (`-1`) and shutting off Friday `same_day` capacity. Separating `r4v_cyc_unblock` (08:00 supervisor) from `r4v_cyc_unblock_backlog` (`backlog_near`) also cured extreme `352`-order surge world `night-15` (`+€1,813.55` and `90.2% -> 91.2%` on-time when `backlog_near` retains `0.25` multiday weight on `cyc_held`).

### What the 16-world `dev` ledger & counters show (`20261114-172144-r4-volume-v7-dev2` vs Champion v7)
- **Ledger (€/wk):** `revenue` rose **`€31,018.06 -> €31,788.40` (`+€770.33/wk`)** while `driver_fee` rose only **`-€18,336.87 -> -€18,802.45` (`-€465.59/wk` = `60.4%` marginal fee/revenue)**; `failed_visit` **improved** from `€-28.49 -> €-18.78/wk` (`+€9.71/wk`).
- **Counters (`/wk`):** `won_ext_ring` rose **`7.62 -> 13.69`** (`won_return_hub = 9.75/wk`, with `r4v_ret_hub_guarded = 213.6/wk` clamping tight periods), `r4v_tradein_won = 1.94/wk` (`~3.9` cars/wk in closed-loop trade-ins), and `lead_car_booked` jumped **`17.81 -> 24.12/wk` (`+6.31` 2-car pairs/wk = `+12.6` cars/wk)** while worst-world `dev` on-time **improved** from **`94.05%` (`dev-14`) to `94.41%` (`dev-13`)**.
- **Negative results that sharpen team knowledge:**
  1. **Non-closed `A->B + A->B` shuttles > 65m (`s4`):** Unlike closed tours (`A->B->A`, 0 empty transits), `A->B + A->B` still incurs two empty `B->A` transits (one mid-gig + one homeward), booking slower at `0.88x` vs `0.85x` solo.
  2. **Quote discounts / lowered slow floors touching `learn()` (`s2`, `dev2`, `v7-dev1`):** Lowering slow floors without `std_floor` neutrality (`s2`) shifted 147 `"floor"` quotes into `"quote"`, stepping `self.mult` up and costing `-17` `same_day` wins on `dev-04`; conversely, skipping `learn()` via `ext_ring_reqs` on frequent hub/closer quotes (`dev2`, `v7-dev1`) starved `self.mult` of loss feedback during competitive weeks (`-13` wins on `dev-16`). Pure gate + pairing + sequential-slack expansion at full tracker price (`v7-dev2`) avoided both traps cleanly.
