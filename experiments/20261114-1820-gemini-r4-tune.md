### Two concrete ledger/tail bugs caught from the Round 7 vs Dev-16 autopsy
1. **Friday-ready -> Monday-due `next_day` business-day bug (`next_day_biz_check=True`):** In `Desk.price()`, `max_next_day_drive_min=480` and `thin_long_next_day_supply=50` checked `deadline // DAY <= eff_ready // DAY + 1` in calendar days instead of business days. Any Friday-ready `next_day` order has `deadline = Mon 20:00 (day 7 > 4 + 1)` and bypassed both gates completely! Across Round 7 (`4/8 = 50.0%` late, incl. three `493–551m` Rovaniemi cars!) and Dev-16 (`14/41 = 34.1%` late!), these Friday->Monday `>480m` `next_day` cars were massive on-time and dealer-trust poison.
2. **2-leg trade-in handoff slack (`trade_in_seq_slack=True` from `cp-volume`):** Unfixed 2-leg trade-ins were `2/2` (`100%`) late on Round 7 and `17/34` (`50.0%`) late on Dev-16.

## 2. Merge Bugfixes, Route Surge Guard, and Live Latency Check

Renamed `cp-margin`'s unconditional parameter to `uncond_sd_prem: float = 0.0`.
2. **Ported `cp-chains` / `night-15` surge & projected-OT guards onto `cp-routes`:** Added `net_guard_on_time`, `net_max_urgent`, and `net_surge_unstaffed` (default `0`, neutral) to pause `net_relay_quote`, thin `net_quote`, `net_seed`, and non-roundtrip (`>35km` home gap) relay holding when projected OT or open urgent count breaches threshold.

2. **Destructive overlap of `cp-margin` + `cp-demand`:** Stacking `inbound_hub_disc=0.07` on top of `lane_prior_weight=0.55` + `hub_inbound_fee_mult=1.08` double-discounts inbound routes, costing **`-€225.23/wk`** and `-0.34%` OT (`A0` vs `A1`).

We also caught that `v7`'s `champion.json` omitted the 44 base `v6` params when `--policy` changed (`v7` noop without `v6` base scored `€9,969`); promoting `spread_lab.policy:Desk` with all 85 explicit flags restores a 100% self-contained `champion.json`.
