## Why we tried it
In wave 2 on Champion v1 (`price_lr=0.02`, `fee_open=1.00`), two ideas showed small positive dev gains: `origin/exp/gemini-clocks` (two price clocks with daily shift reset, `price_lr_day0=0.1`, war-day filter, and regional outage shift: +€45.73 ± 203.70) and `origin/exp/gemini-feefloor` (dense-area opening fee `fee_open_dense=0.85` ramping to `1.0x` plus `night_hold=True` after 17:00: +€104 ± 196). Since Champion v2 promoted the Tier 0 sweep settings (`price_lr=0.05`, `fee_open=0.85`, `offers_per_warning=0`, `min_slack_min=180`), we merged both branches onto v2 (keeping v2's defaults) to test whether either branch's best flags alone, both together, or their non-overlapping sub-mechanisms (`night_hold=True`, `outage_shift=True`) add value on top of v2.

## What changed
Merged `origin/exp/gemini-clocks` and `origin/exp/gemini-feefloor` into `src/spread_lab/policy.py`, resolving conflicts in `Params` by keeping Champion v2's defaults (`fee_open=0.85`, `price_lr=0.05`, `offers_per_warning=0`, `min_slack_min=180`):
- Two clocks (`Params` defaults reproduce v2): `morning_reset: bool = False`, `price_lr_day0: float = 0.15`, `war_shift: float = -0.12`, `shift_cap: float = 0.30`, `outage_shift: bool = False`.
- Fee floor (`Params` defaults reproduce v2): `fee_open_dense: float = -1.0`, `dense_supply: int = 200`, `open_ramp_min_same_day: int = 45`, `open_ramp_min_other: int = 120`, `night_hold: bool = False`.

Commands tested on dev:
- Clocks alone: `uv run lab eval -s morning_reset=True -s outage_shift=True -s price_lr_day0=0.1 -s war_shift=-0.12 --name twoclocks-v2-clocks`
- Feefloor alone: `uv run lab eval -s fee_open_dense=0.85 -s night_hold=True --name twoclocks-v2-feefloor`
- Both together: `uv run lab eval -s morning_reset=True -s outage_shift=True -s price_lr_day0=0.1 -s war_shift=-0.12 -s fee_open_dense=0.85 -s night_hold=True --name twoclocks-v2-both`
- Night hold alone: `uv run lab eval -s night_hold=True --name twoclocks-v2-nighthold`

### Why neither adds to Champion v2 (from `stats` counters)
1. **Champion v2 already captured the core gains of both ideas via Tier 0 sweeps S3 (`fee_open=0.85`) and S4 (`price_lr=0.05`).**
2. **Fee floor (`fee_open_dense=0.85`, `night_hold=True`):**
   - On v1 (`fee_open=1.00`), `fee_open_dense=0.85` lowered opening fees in dense towns (`supply >= 200`). On v2, `fee_open` is already `0.85` everywhere and ramps linearly to `1.60` over the full daytime window until `lastPickupAt - 3h`. Enabling `fee_open_dense=0.85` forces dense gigs up to `1.00x` after just 45 min (`same_day`) or 120 min (other options) via `max(1.0, v0_mult)`, raising mean booked fee ÷ typical fee from `1.012x` to `1.027x`.
   - `night_hold=True` holds fees flat from 17:00 to 08:00 the next day, suppressing v2's evening steps between 17:00 and 22:00 (when drivers still receive notifications until 23:00). This delays bookings (`mean_min_to_book` rises from `383.6` min to `426.6` min on `night_hold=True` alone and `401.8` min on `feefloor`) and increases `deadline_close` warnings from `12.06` per world (v2) to `14.62` (`night_hold`) and `17.50` (`feefloor`), triggering costly steep schedules.
3. **Two clocks (`morning_reset=True`, `outage_shift=True`, `price_lr_day0=0.1`, `war_shift=-0.12`):**
   - On v2 (`price_lr=0.05`), the tracker already adapts rapidly. Enabling `morning_reset=True` disables `loss_streak_freeze=12`, causing the desk to chase price-war days downward (detecting `war_day=1.44` per world).
   - Meanwhile, `outage_shift=True` triggers `0.81` times per world after 8 consecutive wins in high-volume yards (often false positives on normal days), pushing regional prices up and reducing won orders from `227.8` (v2) to `219.5` (-8.3 orders/world).

## Verdict
reject — neither `clocks` (-€366.98 ± 234.65, t = -1.56), `feefloor` (-€274.14 ± 227.47, t = -1.21), both together (-€473.59 ± 196.48, t = -2.41), nor `night_hold=True` alone (-€269.96 ± 149.43, t = -1.81) adds to Champion v2, because v2 already incorporates `price_lr=0.05` and `fee_open=0.85`.
