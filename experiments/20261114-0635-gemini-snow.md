## Why we tried it
1. **R7 & Idea #22 (`snow_guard`):** In `20261114-0130-fable-research-v2-results.md` (Point 11), `dev-07` stood out with 58 `gig_dropped` events on Champion v2 (76 on Champion v3) and 15 late orders on v2 (19 on v3, 94.4% on time), compared with a 16-world median of ~31 drops. Because the rules state that snow slows driving, causes some drivers to stay home and drop bookings that must start that day, and causes summer-tyre cars to fail pickup as `not_ready`, R7 asks whether `forecast` events explain at least a third of `dev-07`'s spike in `gig_dropped`, `visit_failed`, or late orders—and if not, what actually distinguishes `dev-07`'s drops and late orders.
2. **R11 (Hitch & volume pairing for Idea #26):** The top-10 teams on the level-8 leaderboard win 410–460 orders/week vs our ~280–290 on Champion v3. Nearly all of our ~240–290 declined requests per week sit in thin pickup pools (`< 30` drivers within 55 km). Idea #26 (*Hitch*) proposes chaining an unbooked or declined slow order after a won order delivering within 15 km of its pickup (or pairing two declined requests as a round trip) inside the 12-hour multi-car gig limit. R11 measures the exact size of these pools across our revealed dev worlds.

### 4. Answer to R7: Does snow explain at least a third of `dev-07`'s spike in `gig_dropped` or late orders?

**No — snow explains only 5.3% of `dev-07`'s drops (`4/76` on v3, `3/58` = `5.2%` on v2), 0% of `not_ready` visits on v3, and 5.3% of late orders (`1/19` on v3, `0/15` = `0.0%` on v2).** Because this is far below the `>= 1/3` (`33.3%`) threshold, Part 2 (`snow_guard`) is **not** triggered.

Instead, four non-snow mechanisms completely explain `dev-07`'s spike in drops and late orders:
1. **Highest order intake on the smallest driver roster (Monday surge):** `dev-07` has tied-lowest total drivers (`643`, with `325` in the Capital 30 km pool), yet high competitor pricing causes the desk to win **343 orders on v3** (highest of all 16 dev worlds; `84` won on Monday alone vs `63.5` in `dev-05`/`dev-06`). On **Monday (`day=0`)—when there is zero snow anywhere in Finland—`dev-07` already suffers 21 drops on v3** (vs `4.0` in `dev-05`/`dev-06`) and **13 drops on v2** (vs `2.53` across the other 15 worlds).
2. **85.5% of `dev-07` drops are in the Capital + Ring (where it never snows):** On v3, **65 of 76 drops (`85.5%`)** start in `vantaa` (25, `32.9%`), `helsinki` (21, `27.6%`), `hyvinkaa` (10, `13.2%`), `porvoo` (5, `6.6%`), or `espoo` (4, `5.3%`). Because `dev-07`'s board is overloaded with open gigs in the Capital/Ring pool, drivers churn out of low-fee bookings (`42/76 = 55.3%` on v3 and `35/58 = 60.3%` on v2 had `booked_fee / typical_fee <= 0.90`, median `0.882`, median hold time `4.50h` on v3 / `6.59h` on v2).
3. **Wednesday `00:00` midnight Flu spike in the Capital pool:** `dev-07` has **20 drops at `00:00` midnight** on v3 (**8** of them at `Wed 00:00` vs `0.50` in `dev-05`/`dev-06`, plus **6** at `Mon2 00:00` and **3** at `Tue 00:00`), when the weekly **flu** takes 30% of drivers offline for the day and forces them to drop bookings starting that day.
4. **Long-haul (`> 300 min` drive) northern orders (`Oulu`, `Rovaniemi`, `Kemi`, `Ylivieska`):** `dev-07` wins **60** orders with road drive `> 300 min` on v3 (`56` on v2, nearly double the 15-world mean of `32.1`). **25.0% of dropped gigs on v3 (`19/76`, vs `4.1%` in `dev-05`/`dev-06`)** and **32.8% on v2 (`19/58`, vs `14.2%` in other worlds)** are `> 300 min` drives. Crucially, **12 of `dev-07`'s 19 late orders on v3 (`63.2%`)** and **10 of 15 on v2 (`66.7%`)** are `> 300 min` northern deliveries (`oulu` 7, `rovaniemi` 2, `ylivieska` 2, `kemi` 1 on v3) won on Thursday and Friday that drain multi-day driver capacity.

**Key takeaway for Idea #26 (*Hitch*):**
- **Inbound won-order chaining is huge:** On Champion v3, **165.7 declined slow requests/week (`73.2%`)** sit within 15 km of a won order delivering on the same or previous business day with `<= 12h` combined driving, yielding **94.0 disjoint 1-to-1 outbound-then-return chains per week** (`82.3/week` in `< 30 within 55 km` thin towns). At a 35–40% win rate on quoted hitch candidates, that represents **~30–38 additional won orders/week** notified directly to the outbound car's dense pickup pool (300–450 drivers) instead of the thin return town (`< 30` drivers).
- **Declined-to-declined round trips are much smaller:** Only **29.7 declined slow requests/week** on v3 (**26.6/week** across the 16 v2 worlds, or **~11–12 disjoint pairs/week**) pair with another declined slow request as a `<= 12h` round trip, because both ends of a declined-declined pair are in thin towns (`< 30` drivers within 55 km) where neither first pickup notifies a dense driver pool. Thus Idea #26 should prioritize **chaining a thin-pickup return request onto an existing dense-pickup won order** (`94.0` disjoint opportunities/week) rather than pairing two declined thin requests.
