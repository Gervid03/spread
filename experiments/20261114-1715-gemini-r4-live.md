### 2B. Five Concrete Mechanisms Where `play_live` and `play_practice` Decide Differently
1. **Quote `ready` & `slack` clock (`Desk.price`, `policy.py:676`):** `ready = max([self.now] + ...)` uses `self.now`. In `play_practice` (`step_min=10`), a request arriving at `08:01` (`481`) is priced at `self.now = 490`, losing up to **9 minutes of slack** (13m in `fast14`) compared to `play_live` (`self.now = 481`). That explains why `live` wins **+2.6 orders/wk** on v6.
2. **Earlier gig posting & fee schedule anchor (`order_won → post`):** `play_practice` buffers `post` in `outbox` until the next 10m step boundary (0–9m after `respondBy`), whereas `play_live` posts immediately at `respondBy`.
3. **`view_offer_mult` tick cadence (`tick_proactive_offers`):** In `play_live`, `tick()` runs on every event, firing the `1.03×` direct offer at `max(view_at + 2, posted_at + 15)` (`1,990` `offer_answered` on `dev-12`), whereas 10m practice steps check every 10m (`1,607` `offer_answered`) and 14m steps miss ~29% of windows (`1,139` `offer_answered`, explaining part of `fast14`'s `-€414` bias!).
4. **Stepped-practice `dealer_trust` bug (`Desk.handle`, `policy.py:398`):** On `ledger` `revenue`, `handle()` checks `if order and self.now <= order["deadline"]:` instead of `event["at"] <= order["deadline"]` (whereas `rejoin()` checks `max(delivered) <= order["deadline"]`). In `play_live`, `self.now == event["at"]`, so every on-time delivery increments trust; in stepped practice (`fast14` stepping `19:54 → 20:08` across `20:00` deadlines, or night `HOUR` jumps across note deadlines), `self.now > order["deadline"]` silently drops trust increments.
5. **Minor logging bug in `runner.py:66` (`play_live`):** `event.get("type", "?")` should be `event.get("kind", "?")` (`DeskEvent` uses `"kind"`, so live logs currently print `events={'?': ...}`).
