# n14-04-alarm: An early staffing alarm instead of the deadline warning

- **Agent / branch / commit:** gemini · exp/gemini-n14-04-alarm · HEAD
- **Date:** 2026-11-14 00:30
- **Idea from:** IDEAS.md #8

## Why we tried it
In Fable's scouting of v0 worlds, `deadline_close` (3 hours before `lastPickupAt`) was an obituary: 30 of the 42 orders that received `deadline_close` ended late (71.4%), while 75–92% of gigs that booked did so within an hour of posting. By triggering an early staffing alarm when a gig has no driver `alarm_after_min` daytime minutes after its post (`same_day`: the given minutes; `next_day` and `within_3_days`: 10:00 on the deadline day), jumping the fee immediately to `alarm_fee` (1.4×) times the typical fee and climbing on the rising schedule up to `fee_warn` (1.6×), we aimed to staff at-risk orders while drivers still have time to reach the pickup.

## What changed
Added `alarm_after_min: int = 0` (default `0` = off) and `alarm_fee: float = 1.40` to `Params` in `src/spread_lab/policy.py`.
Implemented `Desk.tick`, `Desk.should_alarm`, and `Desk.alarm`, along with `daytime_minutes` and `current_fee` helpers and `rejoin` reconstruction. Added stats counters: `alarms`, `alarms_{option}`, `alarm_to_deadline_close`, `late_alarmed`, `late_deadline_close`, `alarm_fee_cents`, `alarm_fee_gigs`, and `alarm_ref_fee_cents`.

Run with: `uv run lab eval -s alarm_after_min=45 --name n14-04-alarm-dev`

### Alarm, lateness, and fee breakdown
- **Smoke sweep (`30`, `45`, `90` across 4 worlds):**
  - `alarm_after_min=30`: 173 alarms (164 `same_day`, 9 `next_day`), 10 late among alarmed (5.8%), 17 reached `deadline_close` (9.8%), fees paid on alarmed gigs €9,388.36 (€2,347.09/world, 1.430× typical).
  - `alarm_after_min=45`: 131 alarms (124 `same_day`, 7 `next_day`), 7 late among alarmed (5.3%), 15 reached `deadline_close` (11.5%), fees paid on alarmed gigs €7,274.12 (€1,818.53/world, 1.412× typical).
  - `alarm_after_min=90`: 88 alarms (79 `same_day`, 9 `next_day`), 7 late among alarmed (8.0%), 12 reached `deadline_close` (13.6%), fees paid on alarmed gigs €5,139.42 (€1,284.86/world, 1.429× typical).
- **Dev evaluation (`alarm_after_min=45` across 16 worlds vs champion v2):**
  - **Alarms triggered:** 415 total (25.9/world) out of 3,690 won orders — 384 on `same_day` (33.7% of all 1,138 won `same_day` orders) and 31 on `next_day` (1.3% of 2,383 won `next_day` orders).
  - **Late among alarmed vs `deadline_close`:** Only **22 of 415 alarmed orders (5.3%)** ended late, and only 48 of 415 (11.6%) ever reached `deadline_close`, compared against **71.4% (30/42)** late after `deadline_close` in v0 and **32.3% (70/217)** late after `deadline_close` in this run.
  - **Fees paid on alarmed gigs:** €24,090.38 total across 16 worlds (**€1,505.65/world**, €58.05/gig across 415 gigs), or **1.456×** the typical fee (€16,543.61 reference fee).
  - **Why margin fell (`Δmargin €-313.97 ± 180.23`):**
    1. *Interaction with v2's `fee_open=0.85`:* Unlike v0 (`fee_open=1.00`), v2 opens gigs at `0.85×` typical. Because the opening fee is 15% below typical, a third (33.7%) of `same_day` orders remain unbooked at 45 minutes while waiting for the rising schedule to cross ~`1.00×`. Jumping all of them to `1.40×` overpays ~`0.35–0.45×` typical on ~24 `same_day` gigs/world that were not actually in danger of missing their deadline.
    2. *Driver learning (`RULES.md`):* Drivers who view a gig and could do it learn how far its fee rose before booking. Jumping a third of `same_day` gigs to `1.40×` after 45 minutes teaches active drivers to wait for raises, increasing mean `driver_fee` by €428.15/world (`-€17,715.08` vs `-€17,286.93`) and slightly increasing total `deadline_close` warnings across the 16 worlds from 193 to 217 despite mean on-time share rising by `+0.27%` (from `96.75%` to `97.02%`).

## Verdict
reject — `alarm_after_min=45` improves mean on-time share by `+0.27%` with only `5.3%` late among alarmed orders (vs `71%` after `deadline_close`), but overpays `1.456×` typical on `33.7%` of `same_day` gigs under v2's `fee_open=0.85` and teaches drivers to wait, costing `Δmargin €-313.97 ± 180.23` (`t=-1.74`) on dev.
