# cp-wild: Capacity-gated regional return hubs and future-ready return-leg `same_day` unlocking

- **Agent / branch / commit:** gemini (`cp-wild`) · `exp/gemini-cp-wild`
- **Date:** 2026-11-14 10:55 UTC (12:55 Helsinki)
- **Idea from:** own (`cp-wild` diagnosis of weak world `dev-11` via 16-world reveal X-ray + co-play turns & exact `whatif`s)

## Why we tried it
Our focus was **`dev-11`** (where Champion v3 made €7.6k and Champion v3T makes €8,844.58 against a median of €10,418, despite **98.9% on-time** and only 6 deadline warnings — massive unused driver headroom) and finding structural blind spots that none of the other nine co-play tracks cover.

A slot-free reveal autopsy across all 16 Champion dev worlds (`experiments/scripts/xray_cp_wild.py`, `check_mechanisms.py`, `check_trust_coupling.py`) uncovered why `dev-11` and similar low-competitor / regionally skewed Level-8 worlds leave €1k–€1.5k on the table:

1. **Blindness to Level-8 Regional Driver Hubs (`return_hub_min_supply = 200`, `return_hub_max_drive_min = 180`):**
   At Level 8, each town's driver share is multiplied by a world-specific factor (`RULES.md`). On `dev-11`, **Oulu has 118 drivers**, **Tampere 51**, **Jyväskylä 50**, and **Turku/Raisio 41** (similarly, `dev-05` has Jyväskylä 95 & Tampere 72; `dev-01` has Lahti 85 & Turku 63; `dev-07` has Tampere 173; `dev-08` has Oulu 194 & Seinäjoki 73). Hardcoding `return_hub_min_supply = 200` treated **only Helsinki/Espoo/Vantaa** as a return hub and capped drives at `180 min`. Consequently, every return leg from a thin town (`< 22` gate supply, e.g. `Vaasa[3]->Tampere[51] 168m`, `Mikkeli[2]->Tampere[51] 181m`, `Rovaniemi[2]->Oulu[118] 159m`, `Ylivieska[2]->Oulu[118] 96m`, `Kuopio[19]->Vantaa[379] 243m`) was 100% declined — even though drivers living at the destination hub buy a cheap advance outbound bus/train ticket and drive the car home with zero expensive on-the-day return ticket (`HANDBOOK.md`), finishing inside a dense hub ready for their next gig.

2. **The "Calendar-Day vs Label-Day" `same_day` Bug (`future_sameday_slow`):**
   In `RULES.md`, `option["kind"] == "same_day"` means *"ends on the day the car is ready"* — **not** *"ends today"*. Across the 16 Champion dev reveals (`check_mechanisms.py`), **1,022 won 1-car dealer `"same_day"` orders arrived on an earlier day than their ready/deadline day (`deadline // DAY > arrivedAt // DAY`)**, giving drivers 24–80 hours on the gig board to book ahead and a full day to deliver. Their realized lateness was **`0.98%` (`10 / 1,022` = `99.02%` on time — 6× safer than true today-`same_day` at `5.73%` late and 3× safer than `next_day` at `3.03%` late!)** with €32.20 net margin/order. Yet `Desk.price()` blocked `option["kind"] == "same_day"` unconditionally on `pass_return_hub` — declining lucrative multi-day return legs like `R00045 Ylivieska[2]->Espoo[379] (347m, sa: €162 / 33h)` where our co-play probe proved the dealer's real limit was `1.512 × list = €245` vs `€117` ref fee (**€128 margin on one car**).

3. **Destination-Hub Backlog & Tail-Stress Brake (`return_hub_deliv_backlog_ratio`, `return_hub_min_on_time`, `return_hub_max_urgent`, `return_hub_1leg_only`):**
   In `Desk.price()`, thin-town admissions previously checked `backlog_near(pickup)` (`~0` in thin towns) and **never checked `backlog_near(delivery)` at the destination hub whose drivers actually staff the return trip**. Furthermore, 2-car trade-ins across 16 reveals had a **`48.4%` (`15 / 31`) late rate**. On 350+ won-order mega-volume worlds (`night-15`, `night-30`), unthrottled return expansion pushed volume to 364–383 orders (`night-15` dropped from 90.6% to 88.7%). Gating the expanded return intake on 1-car orders, destination-hub backlog `< 0.045 * deliv_supply`, `projected_on_time() >= 0.938`, and `< 4` urgent unstaffed orders (falling back to Champion's `200 / 180` when stressed) preserved **100% of the `+€1,504` gain on `dev-11`** while lifting `night-15` to **90.7% (ranked, above Champion's 90.57%)** and `night-30` to **93.3%**.

## What changed
Added modular, neutral-default fields in `src/spread_lab/policy.py` (`Desk.Params`, `Desk.price`, `Desk.learn`, `Desk.win`, `Desk.rejoin`, plus `quoted_fut_sd`, `won_fut_sd`, `margin_fut_sd_cents`, `late_fut_sd`, and `return_hub_throttled` telemetry), all defaulting to exact Champion v3T behaviour (`smoke` noop `Δ = €+0.00 ± 0.00`):

- `-s return_hub_min_supply=35 -s return_hub_max_drive_min=300`: admits thin-town return legs into regional driver hubs (`>= 35` local drivers within 30 km) up to 300 road minutes.
- `-s future_sameday_slow=true -s fut_sd_return_only=true -s fut_sd_min_deliv_supply=50`: on `pass_return_hub` return legs into `>= 50`-driver hubs, allows 1-car non-private dealer `"same_day"` options whose deadline is on a **future** calendar day (`deadline // DAY > self.now // DAY`, 24–80h board notice), automatically inheriting `ext_ring_neutral_learn=True`.
- `-s return_hub_1leg_only=true -s return_hub_deliv_backlog_ratio=0.045 -s return_hub_min_on_time=0.938 -s return_hub_max_urgent=4`: tail-stress brake that reverts expanded return intake back to Champion's `200 / 180` whenever a destination hub's backlog reaches `4.5%` of its supply, fleet `projected_on_time() < 93.8%`, or `>= 4` urgent orders are unstaffed.
