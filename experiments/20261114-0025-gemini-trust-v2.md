## Why we tried it
Each on-time order for a dealer increases the price that dealer accepts by 1% (up to a count of 25), whereas a late order halves that dealer's count. On champion v1, `trust_invest=0.1` gave `+€215 ± 157` (`t = 1.37`) on dev. With champion v2's faster global price learning (`price_lr=0.05` vs `0.02`), lower opening fees (`fee_open=0.85`), and stricter slack (`min_slack_min=180`), we re-tested whether investing a small discount on safe requests (`supply >= 100`, no note, `slack >= 360` min) for the top 3 dealers by request volume on Monday–Wednesday (until trust reaches 25) compounds better with the faster price tracker.

## What changed
Merged `origin/exp/gemini-trust` onto champion v2 (`Params` fields `trust_gain: float = 0.0`, `trust_invest: float = 0.0`, `trust_guard: bool = False`, plus exact ledger-based dealer trust tracking in `handle()` and `rejoin()`), keeping all champion v2 defaults intact.

Run with: `uv run lab eval -s trust_invest=0.05 --name trustv2-invest0.05`

### Analysis of counters and interactions
- **Why `trust_invest=0.05` works on v2 (`+€303.46 ± 134.52`, `t = 2.26`):** A gentle 5% discount on high-slack, note-free capital orders for the top 3 dealers from Monday to Wednesday accelerates trust accumulation without triggering heavy morning competitor price cuts or giving away too much margin upfront. Across the 16 dev worlds at `trust_invest=0.05`, the top dealer's trust count climbs from a mean of `4.2` at the end of Monday (`d0`) to `14.6` on Tuesday (`d1`) and `24.6` by Wednesday (`d2`, essentially hitting the cap of 25), while mean quoted price ÷ list climbs from `1.437` on Monday to `1.500` on Tuesday, `1.593` on Wednesday, `1.675` on Thursday, and `1.633` on Friday. Mean won orders rise slightly from `227.8` to `231.4`, minimum margin improves from `€6,017.30` to `€6,332.69`, and worst-seed on-time share improves from `94.4%` to `94.7%` (`Δon-time +0.12%`).
- **Why larger discounts (`0.10`, `0.15`) underperform on v2:** With `price_lr=0.05` in v2 (up from `0.02` in v1), the tracker already adapts quickly; a 10% discount sacrifices too much revenue on the safest orders and likely provokes stronger morning competitor price reactions on the top dealers.
- **Why `price_target_win=0.40` is unsafe:** Raising `price_target_win` to `0.40` alongside `trust_invest=0.05` increases volume to `254.9` orders/week, which pushes `dev-07` down to `91.5%` on-time (below the 92% safety floor) and erodes margin (`Δmargin €-11.71`).

## Verdict
adopt `trust_invest=0.05` (keep `price_target_win=0.35`) — **SAFE** (min on-time `94.7%`, ranked 16/16), **Δmargin €+303.46 ± 134.52 (t = 2.26)**, better on 11 of 16 dev seeds.
