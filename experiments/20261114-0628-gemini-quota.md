# quota: A same-day quota per pool instead of a threshold

- **Agent / branch / commit:** gemini · `exp/gemini-n14-05166-quota` · `HEAD`
- **Date:** 2026-11-14 06:28
- **Idea from:** `experiments/IDEAS.md` item #20 ("A same-day quota per pool instead of a threshold", points 7 and 14)

## Why we tried it
In Champion v2 (`same_day_min_supply=100`), `same_day` was stripped from 170–313 requests in 7 of 32 worlds where a busy yard's town kept 30–99 drivers (`IDEAS.md` point 14), while the Capital (~330–440 drivers) had no daily cap (`IDEAS.md` point 7). In Candidate A (`origin/exp/gemini-n14-03324-supply-adaptive-fee-open-and-drop-rescue`), `same_day_min_supply=50` already opened 50–99 driver towns and `ring_to_hub_same_day_min=60` opened short ring-to-hub `same_day` drives, but towns with 35–49 drivers still had `same_day` dropped (`774` options dropped across 16 dev seeds) while the Capital and 50–99 driver towns had no daily `same_day` cap. We tested replacing the static `same_day_min_supply=50` threshold with a pool-proportional daily quota `max(1.0, same_day_quota * supply)` counting today's won `same_day` orders plus `price_target_win` × `same_day` quotes in flight within `supply_radius_km` (30 km), while lowering the minimum pickup supply for `same_day` to `same_day_quota_min_supply=35`.

## What changed
Built on Candidate A (`origin/exp/gemini-n14-03324-supply-adaptive-fee-open-and-drop-rescue`) and added two `Params` fields in `src/spread_lab/policy.py` whose defaults (`0.0`, `35`) reproduce Candidate A and Champion v2 bit-for-bit (`Δmargin €+0.00 ± 0.00` on smoke):
- `same_day_quota: float = 0.0` (0 = off: A's `same_day_min_supply` rule)
- `same_day_quota_min_supply: int = 35`
- When `same_day_quota > 0`, the supply rule for the `same_day` option in `Desk.price()` requires that the pickup has at least `same_day_quota_min_supply` drivers within `supply_radius_km` (or qualifies via `ring_to_hub_same_day_min`), and that `Desk.same_day_load_near(pickup, option["deadline"] // DAY)` (the `same_day` orders won on that deadline day with a pickup within `supply_radius_km`, plus `price_target_win` × unanswered non-declined quotes offering `same_day` there) is strictly less than `max(1.0, same_day_quota * supply)`.
- Tracked `self.same_day_in_flight` (populated when a non-declined quote includes `same_day`, cleared on `order_won` and `order_lost`) and `self.same_day_won` (populated on `win()` for `same_day` orders, cleared on `order_cancelled`, and rebuilt in `Desk.rejoin()` from `snapshot["orders"]`).
- Added counters `dropped_same_day_quota` and `same_day` wins/late orders by pickup driver supply pool size (`>= 200`, `100-199`, `50-99`, `< 50`: `sd_won_200`, `sd_late_200`, `sd_won_100`, `sd_late_100`, `sd_won_50`, `sd_late_50`, `sd_won_thin`, `sd_late_thin`).

### Key Mechanism Findings (Why a Cumulative Daily Quota Hurts on Top of Candidate A)
1. **Candidate A already solved most of Point 14 (`same_day_min_supply` 100 → 50 + `ring_to_hub_same_day_min=60`):**
   - In Champion v2 (`same_day_min_supply=100`), `dev-02`, `dev-10`, and `dev-15` dropped `222`, `215`, and `217` `same_day` options. Lowering `same_day_min_supply` to `50` in Candidate A had already reduced those drops to `45`, `86`, and `57` and raised margin on those three worlds by **+€3,134**, **+€2,291**, and **+€3,006** respectively!
2. **Separating the two effects (`q=0.05, m=50` vs `q=0.06, m=35`):**
   - **Effect of the daily cap alone (`q=0.05, m=50` vs A):** Adding a cumulative daily cap of `max(1.0, 0.05 * supply)` at A's threshold (`m=50`) drops `1,048` `same_day` options (`65.50/w`) and cuts `won_same_day` by `-371` orders (`1,690 → 1,319`), costing **-€711.45 ± 194.79/w** vs A. Why?
     - Because `63–81%` of `same_day` gigs book within 5 minutes of posting (`20261113-2330-fable-research-scouting.md`), Candidate A's instantaneous `backlog_near()` gate (`backlog_multiday_weight=0.25, backlog_in_flight_weight=0.35`) only blocks when orders near the pickup are *actually unstaffed*. A cumulative daily win counter blocks afternoon `same_day` requests even when every morning `same_day` order has already been staffed and delivered.
     - In the Capital (`supply >= 200`), Helsinki, Espoo, and Vantaa lie within 30 km of one another, so `same_day_load_near` sums all three cities' daily `same_day` wins against a single city's 30 km `supply`, throttling Capital `same_day` despite its `1.4–1.7%` late rate (`1,070/15` in `dev1`, `1,026/17` in `dev2`).
     - In ring towns qualifying via `ring_to_hub_same_day_min=60` (e.g., Hyvinkää → Capital), local 30 km `supply` is `< 22`, so `max(1.0, same_day_quota * supply) = 1.0` caps ring-to-hub `same_day` at 1 order per day (`won_ring_hub_same_day` falls from `230` with `1.3%` late in A to `83–89` in `dev1`/`dev2`).
     - When `same_day` is dropped by the quota on a request that also offers `next_day`, the desk quotes `next_day` instead (`won_next_day` rises `+184` in `dev1` and `+244` in `dev2`). Because `next_day` has a ~7% lower list price (`+5%` `same_day_markup` lost) and its fee schedule escalates across the next morning (`driver_fee` rises by `+€419/w` in `dev1` and `+€310/w` in `dev2` despite fewer total orders!), shifting safe `same_day` orders into `next_day` both lowers revenue and increases driver fees.
   - **Effect of lowering the threshold to 35 (`q=0.06, m=35` vs `q=0.05, m=50`):** Lowering `same_day_quota_min_supply` from `50` to `35` cuts `dropped_same_day_supply` from `774` to `461` (`-313` drops) and wins `68` `same_day` orders in `35–49` driver towns (`7` late = `10.3%` late). On `dev-10`, `dropped_same_day_supply` falls from `86` to `3` (`sd_won_thin = 21/1`), gaining `+€396.65` over `q=0.05, m=50`; and on `dev-01` (A's lowest-margin seed), `dropped_same_day_supply` falls from `37` to `0`, beating A by `+€157.18` (`€7,678.55` vs `€7,521.37`) and lifting on-time to `97.95%`. However, this gain is outweighed by the cumulative daily cap's losses in the Capital, `50–99` towns (`dev-02`), and ring-to-hub lanes.

## Verdict
**reject** — Although `same_day_quota=0.06, same_day_quota_min_supply=35` is SAFE (`92.95%` min on-time) and beats Champion v2 by **`Δmargin €+911.68 ± 279.62` (`t = 3.26`, 13/16 better)**, it underperforms Candidate A by **`Δmargin €-675.94 ± 201.25` (`t = -3.36`, 4/12 better/worse)** because Candidate A already lowered `same_day_min_supply` to `50` and gates on instantaneous unstaffed backlog (`backlog_near`), whereas a cumulative daily `same_day` quota needlessly caps high-margin, fast-booking `same_day` volume in the Capital (`1.4%` late) and ring-to-hub lanes (`1.3%` late) and shifts them into dearer `next_day` gigs.
