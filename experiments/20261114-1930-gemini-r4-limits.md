## Journal & Why We Tried It

### 1. Slot-free X-ray of the TAS Price Oracle (`experiments/scripts/analyze_r4_limits.py`, `analyze_r4_deep.py`, `sim_r4_limits.py`, `xray_world_places.py`)
Before spending a single practice slot, we analyzed all 1,833 exact `(request, option)` reservation limits on `dev-12` (`limits-all.jsonl`) and 1,138 won-order limits across `dev-12`, `dev-01`, `dev-08`, and `dev-07` (`€20,068.22` total oracle headroom = **`€5,017.06/world`, `+€17.63/order`**):
1. **Exact Multi-Option Dealer Choice Law (`29/29 = 100.0%` exact on `dev-12`)**:
   - When multiple options are quoted (`{"same_day": P_sd, "next_day": P_nd}`), dealers choose `argmax_opt (lim_opt - P_opt)` whenever `max(lim_opt - P_opt) >= 0`.
   - Crucially, for **64% (`51/80`)** of `same_day` winners, `P_nd <= lim_nd` is **False** (the dealer is speed-sensitive and rejects `next_day`). Counterfactual simulation proved that blindly hiking `same_day` markup by `+5%` loses 14 speed-sensitive orders and `-€262/wk`.
2. **Decomposition of the €5,017/wk Oracle Gap & the Idiosyncratic Hazard Trap**:
   - Controlling for `(day, dealer)`, exact trust `(1 + 0.01 * trust)`, and static 64-world lane effects leaves within-`(day, dealer)` log-limit dispersion `sigma = 0.1166` (`0.0905` within `batchId`, `0.0686` after world-specific pickup + delivery random effects, with `corr(MonTue, WedThu) = +0.702` across corridors).
   - Under `sigma = 0.117` and `€45–€80` gross margin per won order, upward price hikes face a ~2.6-to-1 adverse Mills-ratio trade-off whenever the fleet has spare capacity: hiking prices into request-level noise loses `~3.3%` of quotes per `+1%` hike.
   - Testing upward hikes on smoke confirmed this exact trap:
     - **`s1-events` (`lim_outage_mult=1.20` + `lim_war_snapback=1.0`)**: `-€434.02 ± 264.58` (`t = -1.64`) on smoke — throttling regional outage win share down toward `45%` threw away 19 `€80`-margin outage cars on `dev-03` (`-€1,014`), and snapping `mult` up `+34%` on post-war mornings threw away 12 fleet-refill cars on `dev-04` (`-€750`), while **Friday war unfreezing (`lim_fri_war_unfreeze`)** gained `+€50.79` cleanly on `dev-02`.
     - **`s2-dealer` (`lim_comp_step=0.035` + `lim_deadband_bump=0.08`)**: lost `-€542` on `dev-02` and `-€752` on `dev-04` because dealers with `<30%` win share (`D02..D05`) already had low world-specific dealer factors (`~0.90x`); stepping `dealer_adj` up `+3.5%` at `08:00` undid yesterday's downward learning and locked us out of those dealers all week.
     - **`s5-world-lane` (symmetric online `pick_adj * deliv_adj * pair_adj` in `[0.88, 1.14]`)**: gained `+€597` on congested `dev-07` and `+0.64%` on-time across 8 seeds, but lost `-€166.63 ± 153.84` overall because `w_lane > 1.0` cannibalized `14–21` high-margin outbound cars on uncongested `dev-03` and `dev-04`.

### 2. What Worked — and Why Cycle-Desk (`v7`) Unlocked Its Full Value
Three zero-drift, congestion-safe live oracle mechanisms emerged cleanly from the ablations:
1. **`lim_corridor=True` — Cycle-Aware Inbound Corridor & Floor Pricing + Multi-Option Floor Spread**:
   - Uses strictly out-of-sample 64-world Empirical-Bayes shrunk `LIM_LANE_PICKUP` / `LIM_LANE_DELIVERY` priors (`night-01..32 + final-01..32`; zero `dev` or `holdout` data) to discount negative-`eff` inbound routes (`lim_lane_neg_w = 0.55`) with balanced `Desk.learn()` slope compensation (`lim_lane_comp_slope = 1.85`) so global `self.mult` neither drifts upward (which cost 21 cars on `dev-16` at `1.50`) nor drifts downward (which cost `-5%` on `dev-08`/`dev-15` at `2.20`).
   - Lowers the Inbound-to-Hub (`supply < 200 -> supply >= 200`) minimum-margin floor from `1.25x ref / 0.92` to `1.12x ref / 0.92` (`lim_inb_fee_mult = 1.12`), protecting solo margin on unpartnered inbound cars while rescuing profitable inbound return legs that **close held outbound cars into `0.48x sum(ref)` 2-car cycles and `0.44x sum(ref)` 3-car triangles on `cycles:Desk`**.
   - **Critical Cycle-Desk & Supervisor Synergy Fixes**:
     - Excludes intentionally held/staged cycle pool cars (`cyc_held`, `staged_orders`) from `unstaffed_now` congestion counting — previously, holding 15 outbound cars waiting for a cycle tripped `unstaffed > 16` and shut off the exact inbound discounts needed to close those cycles!
     - On Thursday afternoons (`>= 12:00`), caps inbound discounting at `unstaffed <= sup_fri_max_backlog - 1` (`4`) so Thursday inbound orders never trip `cp-supervisor`'s Friday `08:00` `unstaffed <= 5` surplus gate.
     - Enforces `lim_floored_sd_prem = 0.10` when `next_day` hits the floor so multi-option floor quotes never collapse `same_day` onto `next_day`.
2. **`lim_dealer=True` — Zero-Drift Centered Exact Dealer Trust + Mid-Week Cold-Join Calibration**:
   - Applies `exp(0.0030 * (dealer_trust[d] - lim_trust_ema))` at Level 8, harvesting high-trust `D01` (`trust = 25`, `Med Lim/Q = 1.153`) while relieving `trust = 0` dealers (`D02..D40`) with zero drift on `self.mult`, and dropping `D01`'s quote immediately if a late delivery halves trust.
   - Adds `lim_cold_join_mult = 1.50` upon mid-week cold starts (`now >= 2 * DAY`, `0` orders, `>= 250` prior lost requests — the exact `Thu 00:00` Test Final regime where 3 days of `0%` share give `+3` competitor morning hikes). Strict no-op on Monday starts; adds **`+€115.77` (`€2,515.10 -> €2,630.87`, `97.4% -> 98.6%` OT)** on `dev-12` with `live_from_min=4320`.
3. **`lim_events=True` — Healthy-Board Friday Price-War Unfreeze**:
   - Unfreezes `loss_streak_freeze` on Friday (`day == 4`) when `unstaffed <= 8` and `projected_on_time >= 0.945` (while keeping the hard margin floor active), harvesting profitable weekend-staffed slow orders during Friday price wars instead of freezing after 12 losses.
