## Why we tried it
The dealer counts our on-time orders exactly and accepts prices +1% per count. A late order halves that count. So we can invest trust to get a higher volume of safe requests, or charge higher prices.

## What changed
Added `trust_gain`, `trust_invest`, and `trust_guard` to `Params`.
`trust_gain` multiplies the quote by `1 + trust_gain * count / 100`.
`trust_invest` applies a discount for safe requests to the top 3 dealers Monday-Wednesday.
`trust_guard` declines risky requests (same_day in thin towns or > 300 min drives) for high trust dealers.
Rebuilt trust counts faithfully in `rejoin()` from history.

Run with: `uv run lab eval -s trust_gain=1.0 -s trust_invest=0.1 -s trust_guard=False --name trust`

`trust_gain=1.0` alone is statistically worse on dev (-285, t=-2.43). The manual price increase causes us to lose quotes and the global tracker then mistakenly assumes we are too expensive globally, bringing down the prices everywhere else.
`trust_invest=0.1` alone is slightly positive on dev (+215, t=1.37) but not statistically significant enough to claim an improvement.
`trust_guard=True` alone lost €711 on smoke because it drops high-revenue `same_day` orders in thin towns that we were otherwise safely fulfilling.

## Verdict
reject — `trust_gain` conflicts with the global win tracker, and `trust_invest` is not strong enough to meet the threshold.
